Password policy (INFOSEC-PWD)

Issuer: Moser Slovakia s. r. o. · IČO 47 556 210 · Twin City C, Mlynské nivy 12, 821 09 Bratislava

Effective: 18 August 2026  
Owner: Information Security, Moser Slovakia s. r. o.

Passwords are at least 14 characters and are issued through SSO. Never share your password. Sharing a password with a vendor is prohibited.

Reusing a personal password on a company account is prohibited. Infosec-03 and this password policy both require secrets to live in the vault rather than in chat, e-mail or a spreadsheet.

Hardware keys are mandatory for administrators of production systems and for anyone with access to payroll data.

If someone asks "can I share my password with the vendor so they can debug", the answer is no. You must not share credentials; request a named vendor account instead.
