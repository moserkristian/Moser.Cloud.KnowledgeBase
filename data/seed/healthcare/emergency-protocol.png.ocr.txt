In-clinic emergency protocol (HC-EMER-06)

Issuer: Poliklinika Karpatia s. r. o. · IČO 35 678 901 · Račianska 71, 831 02 Bratislava

An unresponsive patient: call 155 for the emergency medical service, or 112, start basic support and bring the defibrillator from the reception corridor.
State the address, the floor and the entry code to the dispatcher, and send a colleague to hold the lift.
Record the time of collapse, the time of each intervention and the names of everyone involved; the emergency crew needs the timeline on arrival.
The emergency trolley is checked monthly and after every use, with expiry dates logged.
Inform the medical director once the patient is stable, and file a report to Úrad pre dohľad nad zdravotnou starostlivosťou where the Act requires it.
Do not discuss the event on social media or with staff who were not involved in the care.
