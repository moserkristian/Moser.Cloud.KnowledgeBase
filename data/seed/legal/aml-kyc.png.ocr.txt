AML and client intake (LEG-AML-07)

Issuer: Advokátska kancelária Hoferová & Bartoš s. r. o. · IČO 51 234 567 · Laurinská 12, 811 01 Bratislava

An advocate is an obliged entity under the AML Act (zákon č. 297/2008 Z. z.) when handling property transactions, company formations or funds held for a client.
Identify the client and the beneficial owner before receiving any funds; verify the beneficial owner against the Business Register and, for public sector counterparties, the Register of Public Sector Partners.
Enhanced due diligence applies to a retainer above EUR 15,000, to a politically exposed person, and to any counterparty in a high-risk third country.
Cash payments are capped at EUR 5,000 by zákon č. 394/2012 Z. z., so a cash retainer above that limit is refused outright.
Report an unusual business operation to the Financial Intelligence Unit of the National Crime Agency without delay, and do not tip off the client.
Engaging a politically exposed person requires senior partner approval recorded in the file.
