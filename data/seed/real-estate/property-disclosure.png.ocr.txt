Seller disclosure (RE-DISC-05)

Issuer: Dunaj Reality s. r. o. · IČO 46 891 203 · Štúrova 8, 811 02 Bratislava

The seller must disclose known defects that make the property unfit for its purpose: roof and structural damage, damp and water ingress, pests, and any unauthorised building work.
A defect concealed at sale gives the buyer a claim for liability for defects under the Civil Code, and the agency is exposed alongside the seller.
Check the land register extract for easements, liens and enforcement notes, and check the building authority file for a valid occupancy permit before publishing the listing.
Where the flat sits in a building with an owners' association, unpaid contributions to the repair fund transfer with the unit and must be disclosed.
Agents must never advise a seller to conceal a defect, and must not describe an unpermitted extension as living area.
