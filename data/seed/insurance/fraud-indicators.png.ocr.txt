Fraud indicators (INS-FRD-04)

Issuer: Poisťovňa Devín, a. s. · IČO 00 682 447 · Pribinova 10, 811 09 Bratislava

Indicators that route a claim to the special investigation unit: a loss reported long after the event, a narrative that changes between statements, damage inconsistent with the described mechanism, cover taken out shortly before the loss, and a claims history clustered across related parties.
Cross-check the vehicle in the motor insurers' database and the claims register before settling a total loss.
Never state or imply fraud in correspondence with the insured. Use neutral investigation wording and let the file speak.
The unit may hold payment while the investigation runs, but the statutory investigation deadline still applies, so the delay must be justified in the file.
Where the evidence supports it, the insurer files a criminal complaint for insurance fraud under §223 of the Criminal Code; that decision belongs to legal, not to the handler.
Every referral is documented with a timestamp and the indicator that triggered it.
