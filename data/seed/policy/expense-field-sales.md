# Expense policy — field sales (FIN-EXP-50)

Effective: 4 February 2026  
Owner: Field Finance

For field sales roles only, receipts are required for expenses **over $50**. This higher threshold does **not** apply to corporate staff. Per-diem meals still need an itemized receipt when the meal exceeds $50.

## Collision note
Employees often quote $25 vs $50 interchangeably. Check whether the submitter is on the field-sales cost center before answering.

Cash tips above $20 still need a note even when under the receipt threshold.
