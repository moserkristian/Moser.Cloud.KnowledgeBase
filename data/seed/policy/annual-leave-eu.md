# EU annual leave (HR-AL-EU)

Effective: 1 January 2026  
Owner: People Operations (EU)

EU employees receive **25 days** of statutory annual leave, plus public holidays of the employing country. This is not US PTO. Contractors on 1099-equivalent arrangements are not covered.

## Booking
- Leave is booked in the local HRIS, not Workday PTO.
- Carry-over is limited to 5 days unless a works-council agreement says otherwise.

Do not tell EU staff they have 15 PTO days. Do not tell US staff they have 25 days of annual leave.
