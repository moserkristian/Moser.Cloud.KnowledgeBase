# Returns policy (OPS-RET-14)

Effective: 12 January 2026  
Owner: Warehouse Operations

Physical returns of catalog goods follow the **14-day** RMA process. An RMA number is required before the carrier pickup. This document is named similarly to refund policies but covers logistics, not cash.

## RMA steps
1. Customer requests RMA in the portal.
2. Warehouse issues a label valid for 14 days.
3. Goods arriving after label expiry are refused.

Do not quote Premium 30-day subscription refunds as a returns window. Returns ≠ refunds.
