# Premium refund — enterprise subscriptions (FIN-REF-30)

Effective: 1 March 2026  
Owner: Finance Operations  
Applies to: Premium Support and Enterprise subscription SKUs only.

Premium customers may request a refund within **30 days** of the subscription start date. This 30-day window does **not** apply to standard catalog SKUs.

## Conditions
- Unused seats may be refunded pro-rata within 30 days.
- Once named users have been provisioned for more than 7 days, seat refunds require a Premium CSM ticket.
- Professional services already delivered are non-refundable.

## Collision note
Support agents often confuse this 30-day Premium rule with the 14-day standard catalog refund. Always check the SKU class before quoting a window.
