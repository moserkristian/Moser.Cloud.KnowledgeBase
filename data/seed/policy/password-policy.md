# Password policy (INFOSEC-PWD)

Effective: 18 August 2026  
Owner: Information Security

Passwords are at least 14 characters via SSO. **Never share** your password. Password sharing with a vendor is prohibited.

Reuse of personal passwords is prohibited. Infosec-03 and this password policy both require vaulted secrets instead of chat.

If asked "can I share my password with the vendor so they can debug", the answer is no. Must not share credentials.
