# Vendor onboarding (PROC-VON)

Effective: 2 May 2026  
Owner: Procurement

New vendors need: W-9/VAT id, security questionnaire, and a commercial owner inside the company. Access is not granted during onboarding until Security signs the questionnaire.

Standing shared logins are prohibited. Vendor engineers receive named accounts after onboarding completes.

Do not confuse this document with vendor **offboarding**. Onboarding grants access only after approval.
