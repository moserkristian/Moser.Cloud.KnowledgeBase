# Travel policy (OPS-TVL)

Effective: 9 March 2026  
Owner: Workplace

Book flights and hotels through the designated corporate agency. Economy is the default under 6 hours. Business class requires VP approval. Personal travel combined with business travel must be priced as a comparison screenshot attached to the ticket.

Rental cars: compact or midsize only. GPS and insurance add-ons from the counter are not reimbursable when corporate coverage already applies.
