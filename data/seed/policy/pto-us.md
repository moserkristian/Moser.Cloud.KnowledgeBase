# US paid time off (HR-PTO-US)

Effective: 1 January 2026  
Owner: People Operations (US)

Full-time US employees accrue **15 days** of PTO per calendar year. PTO is distinct from EU annual leave. Unused PTO above 5 days does not cash out except at termination where required by state law.

## Accrual
- Year 0–2: 15 days.
- Managers cannot grant "unlimited PTO" informally.
- Sick time is tracked separately in Workday.

PTO requests over 5 consecutive days need manager approval 14 days in advance.
