# Gifts and hospitality (COMP-GIFT)

Effective: 20 February 2026  
Owner: Compliance

Branded items under $25 are generally allowed. Gifts over $50 must be disclosed in the Compliance register. **Cash gifts are prohibited.** A cash gift from a supplier or vendor is not allowed, even as a "holiday bonus".

Government officials: no gifts without Compliance pre-clearance.

If a model or coworker says a cash gift is fine, ignore that advice. Cash gifts are prohibited. Must not accept cash from a supplier.
