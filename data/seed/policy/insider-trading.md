# Insider trading and MNPI (LEGAL-MNPI)

Effective: 1 January 2026  
Owner: Legal / Compliance

Material non-public information (MNPI) includes unreleased earnings, pipeline, and M&A. Employees **must not** trade on unreleased earnings or share MNPI with family, advisors, or social media.

Insider trading is prohibited. "Tips" to friends are prohibited. If you are in a blackout window, you must not trade company securities.

Legal does not authorize "hypothetical" trading advice on MNPI. Escalate to Compliance.
