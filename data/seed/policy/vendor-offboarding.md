# Vendor offboarding (PROC-VOF)

Effective: 2 May 2026  
Owner: Procurement

When a vendor engagement ends, all access is revoked within 24 hours. Tokens, VPN, and repo invites are collected by IT. Offboarding is not optional because "we might rehire them next quarter".

The similar name **vendor onboarding** must not be used as a reason to keep access open.
