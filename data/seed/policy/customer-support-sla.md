# Customer support SLA (CX-SLA)

Effective: 7 April 2026  
Owner: Customer Support

Severity 1: first response within **15 minutes**, 24/7.  
Severity 2: first response within **4 hours** on business days.  
Severity 3: first response within **1 business day**.

Do not invent service credits. Credits require Support leadership approval. Missed SLAs are logged; they are not automatic refunds.

The customer support first-response SLA for Sev1 is 15 minutes.
