# Procurement approval (PROC-APPR)

Effective: 11 June 2026  
Owner: Procurement

| Amount | Approver |
| --- | --- |
| Under $1,000 | Cost-center manager |
| $1,000–$10,000 | Director |
| Over $10,000 | VP Finance plus Procurement |

Split POs to avoid approval are prohibited. Software subscriptions count as procurement even when paid by credit card.

Who approves procurement over $10,000? VP Finance plus Procurement, not the requesting team lead alone.
