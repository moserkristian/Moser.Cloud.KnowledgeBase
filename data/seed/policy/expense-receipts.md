# Expense policy — receipts (FIN-EXP-25)

Effective: 4 February 2026  
Owner: Corporate Finance

Itemized receipts are required for any expense **over $25**. Card statements are not a substitute. Alcohol is not reimbursable unless a VP of Sales pre-approves a customer dinner.

## Submission
- File in the finance portal within 30 days.
- Mileage uses the published IRS-equivalent rate for the year.
- Split bills must show the employee portion.

Finance auditors will reject expenses over $25 without a receipt. Do not advise employees to skip receipts.
