# Standard refund — catalog SKUs (FIN-REF-14)

Effective: 1 March 2026  
Owner: Finance Operations  
Applies to: standard catalog SKUs sold B2B in the EU and US.

Customers may request a refund within **14 days** of delivery for unopened catalog SKUs. The 14-day window is measured from the carrier scan that marks the shipment delivered.

## Conditions
- Item must be unused and in original packaging.
- Digital licenses and custom-cut hardware are excluded.
- Restocking is not charged when the return is our error.

## How to file
Open a ticket in the Finance portal with order id, SKU, and delivery date. Do not promise a 30-day window for standard SKUs. The 14-day rule is the controlling policy for this product class.

Managers must not override this window in chat. Exceptions go to Finance, not Support.
