# Remote work policy (HR-REMOTE)

Effective: 1 May 2026  
Owner: People Operations

Hybrid default is 3 days in office unless a written remote agreement exists. Core collaboration hours are 10:00–15:00 local. Managers cannot waive MFA, disk encryption, or VPN requirements for remote staff.

Working from a country not listed in the employment contract requires Mobility + Legal approval. Do not advise "just use a tourist visa".
